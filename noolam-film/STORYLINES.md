# NOOLAM horizontal film: three storylines

Facts the film may use: NOOLAM (showcase brand), cult91.com photography and the cut-outs made from it, real product names and prices from `noolam/js/data.js`, and the site's own lines: "Woven for the Divine.", "An heirloom from the first fold.", "Try the Look.", "Three silks, one weave.", "Woven with heritage. Draped for today."

Assumptions: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 20–30 s. It is made to loop muted in the site's hero and drape screen, so the default is no music. Optional: a music bed from the connected ElevenLabs account (uses its credits) for a cut you can share; the free option is silent.

## A. One Thread (recommended: it loops seamlessly in the site's muted video slot and follows the site's own order)
- **Device / form:** one object across worlds (a single gold-red thread) / one continuous take with endless zooms. The viewer follows one thread from the loom to the finished look, so the whole brand reads as one unbroken weave.
- **First 2 s:** a macro of orange-tussar stripes sliding left. One gold thread lifts off the cloth and pulls the camera after it.
- **Beats:** thread lifts → dive into the cloth → pull back to the saree on a model → the model joins a lineup of eight on white ("Try the Look.") → the lineup slides into the showroom ("Woven for the Divine.") → three fabrics braid into the thread ("Three silks, one weave.") → NOOLAM crest and wordmark woven in → the thread exits frame left, ready to loop.
- **Look:** white ground #FFFFFF, ink #1D1B20, crimson #C8102E rationed to one word per line; Inter Tight 600 titles at 96–120 px, NOOLAM in Cormorant Garamond 600; the material is the real silk photography and soft cut-out shadows.
- **Sound:** silent by default (plays muted on the site). Optional bed: slow tanpura drone with a soft tabla entering at the braid, 80 bpm.

## B. Pick a Silk (the bold one)
- **Device / form:** type carries the argument / hard cuts on the beat. Full-bleed colour fields (rani pink, mustard, peacock green, sindoor, royal blue, violet) cut every half beat, each with a giant condensed word and the model cut-out slamming in over it.
- **First 2 s:** a rani-pink field. "RANI" hits at 380 px, the rani-pink suit cut-out drops over it, and it cuts to mustard on the next half beat.
- **Beats:** six colour cuts → a white pause, "Woven for the Divine." → the eight sarees step forward one by one → the three ribbons braid → NOOLAM end card on crimson.
- **Look:** full-saturation fields from the site's Pick a Silk cards; Anton caps 300–420 px in cream #FFF4D4; cut-outs with a hard 20% drop shadow; Inter Tight for the one white beat.
- **Sound:** silent by default. Optional: 120 bpm dhol and bass with a hit on every cut and one riser into the braid.

## C. Six Days to the Muhurtham
- **Device / form:** a moment in someone's day (a bride choosing her saree) / a flight through one world, the showroom as a set the camera glides across.
- **First 2 s:** the store front, warm light. A time stamp types in: "Saturday, 11:00 · six days to the muhurtham", and the camera starts to glide in.
- **Beats:** time stamp → glide along the rail (pieces swing as the camera passes) → three looks tried (cut-outs with numbered hotspots) → the bridal arches fan open, one is chosen ("Rust Drape · ₹6,599") → artisans' hands ("An heirloom from the first fold.") → NOOLAM end card.
- **Look:** the showroom photo as the home stage, white panels for the try-on; Inter Tight 600; crimson only on the chosen piece and the end card.
- **Sound:** silent by default. Optional: shehnai-led bed at 72 bpm, swelling at the chosen saree.
